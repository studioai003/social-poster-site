Privacy policy

Social Poster posts videos to accounts whose owner has connected them. This page says what it stores and how to stop it.

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What we store

  • The access key that a service gives us when an owner connects an account. It lets us post to that account and read the list of its own posts.
  • The name and ID of the connected account or channel.
  • The videos, titles and captions the owner gives us to post, and a log of when each was posted.

We do not store passwords. We do not collect data about viewers, followers or anyone else.

What we use it for

Only to post the owner's approved videos to the owner's own account, and to check that what we posted is there. We do not sell data, share it with third parties or use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Access keys are kept as encrypted secrets with our hosting provider, GitHub. They never appear in our files, logs or dashboards.

YouTube

Social Poster uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove our access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Instagram, Facebook and Threads

You can remove our access at any time in your Facebook settings under "Business integrations", and in your Instagram or Threads settings under "Apps and websites".

Deleting your data

Removing access stops all posting at once. To have the stored access key, account name and posting log deleted as well, write to Leximonroe.0003@gmail.com. We delete them within 30 days and confirm by email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
