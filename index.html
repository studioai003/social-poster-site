Social Poster

A private publishing tool. It posts short videos to the social media accounts of the people who run it, on a schedule they approve.

What it does

The owner of an account connects it once. After that, Social Poster uploads the videos that the owner has approved to that account, at the planned time, and keeps a log of what was posted.

Who it is for

Social Poster is used by its own team for the accounts they own or manage. It is not open for public sign-up.

Which services it connects to

YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok, each through the official interface of that service and only with the permission of the account owner.
